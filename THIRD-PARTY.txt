BetterVR by Crementif and contributors: MIT license and copyright notice in
licenses/BetterVR-MIT.txt. BetterVR was an important technical reference for
the stereo render flow, GPU marker protocol, shadow reuse, camera/projection
research and HUD separation. See CREDITS.md.

Khronos OpenXR loader: Apache 2.0 license text in
licenses/OpenXR-Apache-2.0.txt. The loader is linked into the VR layer.

JsonCpp: MIT license and copyright notice in licenses/JsonCpp-MIT.txt. JsonCpp
source may be incorporated into the statically linked OpenXR loader depending
on how that loader was built.

Vulkan headers and Windows SDK/D3D11 system libraries are build dependencies.
The community Super Mario 3D World resolution graphic pack by Crementif and
getdls is used in the documented setup but is not redistributed here.
